## Filing contract

Public world presentation is protected during filing: preserve world links, Featured world cards, covers and existing published media. CASSANDRA must remain reachable at `/w/cassandra`. Keep internal style locks and audit notes in private Stories/Scripts, rather than replacing public page copy. Verify `showcase.getWorld` and `showcase.getShowcaseHome.worlds` after changing a published world's organization; unchanged asset visibility flags alone do not prove its page still works.

1. Determine source/provenance, visual medium and exact style from the actual image or video, not filename or auto-description. Source .webp saves stay in INSPIRATION VAULT and never enter a world or storybook, following Michael's explicit AGENTS rule. This applies to original/source WebP media; a derived WebP card thumbnail does not turn a PNG/JPEG/video production asset into a WebP save.
2. Pinterest is inspiration only. Keep assetRole inspiration_capture and the inspiration tag. Use character-design or environment-design for external references rather than claiming production character/location/scene. Do not file Pinterest into canonical worlds or storybooks; use inspiration collections and optional world-ann-reference, world-dear-annete-reference or world-daddy-retro-reference tags. Storytell references may share the channel-development collection while retaining a reference-library membership.
3. Own production assets go into their correct world part with piece type, medium and exact style tags. Character means a person/creature; carriage means vehicle; garment-only studies mean costume-study. Interaction in-frame is a scene, rather than an empty location plate.
4. Styles are tags, not folders. Animation Development describes production readiness, not a new style family. Keep one exact style/identity/costume inside each storybook even when its world supports several style tags.
5. The gallery's live-action visual filter also includes photoreal rendered output. It does not mean an asset was photographed. Actual photos remain external inspiration, and photoreal rendered locations remain separate from primary LIZ animation.
6. Keep source motion clips, identity sheets, inspiration captures, story frames and generated output distinguishable. Do not claim a style-reference clip is a finished shot for a new story. Complete source crops conservatively and declare inferred lower costume details.
7. Save text-only ideas, scripts, critiques and evolving style locks as native Stories/Scripts records, linked to the intended collection/storybook. Keep revision history. Collection descriptions summarize current locks; the native style-lock text holds the detailed constraints and anchor IDs.
8. Open stories on action already happening, create tension or a recognizable feeling immediately, and develop a concrete choice. Remove mismatching references instead of forcing a mixed-style cast. Explicitly mark missing matching cast/locations as gaps.
9. Never silently change public, featured or liked flags during filing. Preserve unique media and full source/prompt provenance. Use newest reads and minimal membership/tag deltas so concurrent storybook or sheet work is retained.
10. Avoid naming a collection after a master filter pill (Animation, Characters, Locations, Scenes, Design). The established Dear animation part is Animated. New menu presets are owner-curated, not a raw tag cloud.
