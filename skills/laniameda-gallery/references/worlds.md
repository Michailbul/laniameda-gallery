# Worlds: what is what

Michael's story universes, as they live in the gallery since 30 Sep 2026. Read this
before filing, pulling references for, or writing about any of these worlds. When the
gallery and this file disagree, check the gallery (`list_collections` / query `list` with
`folderId`) and fix this file.

A **world** is a root collection. Its folders (one level deep) are **parts** of the world:
a format or a direction. Inside a part, the visual **style** is a tag, so styles never mix
silently. Piece type (`character` / `location` / `scene`) and medium (`animation`) are tags
as everywhere else.

### DEAR ANNETE
A dystopian-utopian baroque fantasy cyberpunk romance. Futuristic-retro Prague, year 2443:
gothic spires, red tiled roofs, copper chimneys, brass lanterns, airships, baroque
aristocracy fused with cybernetics, gilded upper city over a dark underbelly. Tone: quiet,
tender, a little sad (the tone model is the short "The Cold Hand",
`~/AI-video-work/Dear Annette/rooftop-story-2026-09-30/the-cold-hand-prompt.txt`).

Leads:
- **The young man**: freckled, swept-back black hair, black high-collared double-breasted
  military coat with a short cape and brass buttons, segmented **gold mechanical LEFT arm**.
  Only exact match in the gallery: `asset:j573wn34grkc351avtxc5x1m598axans`. Near misses
  (not him): both arms gold, no gold arm, gold RIGHT arm.
- **The woman**: pale, long straight black hair, pale blue eyes, dark lips, black lace choker
  with a silver pendant, white ruffled off-shoulder blouse, deep red puffed sleeves, dark red
  corset. **No reference of her in this costume exists yet.** Don't substitute a random
  dark-haired woman; Michael rejected loose matches.

  (also the world cover). Anchor scene: 30-s rooftop video `asset:j57aqw4rf92x4h6503v1gkwwb98f4gay`.

## Filing a new piece into a world

1. Decide the world by **story world and style together**. A piece that fits the world but
   not any of its looks goes nowhere yet: ask Michael, or leave it unfiled.
2. File into the world's **part** (the folder), never loose in the DEAR ANNETE root.
3. Tag: piece type, `animation` when animated, and the part's **style tag** from the tables
   above. Reuse these exact tag names; ask before starting a new style.
4. `.webp` → INSPIRATION VAULT, never a world.
5. Storybooks (`kind: storybook`) combine a world's characters, locations and beats; keep a
   storybook inside one world and one style.
