## Caveat

These runs attached to Michael's signed-in Chrome profile, which carries two extensions (the
gallery's own Midjourney dock and one more). Lighthouse counts their injected scripts: the 363
and 266 KiB "unused JavaScript" rows and part of the "Unattributable" long tasks are extension
cost, not the app. The app's own cost is still plain: chunk `3794-*.js` alone scripts for
2.1–2.9 s on a mid phone, the dashboard ships 1.5 MB of JavaScript, and `main#gallery-main-content`
shifts the whole page once (CLS 0.545). A clean signed-out headless run follows below.
